Electronic Components

Resistor

A resistor limits the flow of electric current.

Capacitor

A capacitor stores electrical energy.

Diode

A diode allows current to flow mainly in one direction.

Transistor

A transistor works as a switch or amplifier.

Inductor

An inductor stores energy in a magnetic field.

LED

An LED produces light when current flows through it.
